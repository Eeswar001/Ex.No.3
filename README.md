Procedure:
1. Straightforward Prompts:
These prompts directly ask a question or request a simple explanation or list.

Examples:
    a) “Define photosynthesis in one sentence.”
    b) “List three advantages of electric vehicles.”
    c) “Explain how AI is used in monitoring air pollution.”
    d) “State the importance of renewable energy in environmental protection.”

2. Tabular Format Prompting:
This type of prompting requests data or comparison in a structured table format for better clarity and understanding.

Examples:
    a) “Compare and contrast AC and DC current in a table.”
    b) “Provide a table listing five programming languages, their paradigms, and one use case each.”
    c) “Create a table showing the applications of AI in different environmental sectors.”

3. Preceding Question Prompting:
This prompting technique asks a sequence of related questions where the answer to one leads to another, building logical flow and depth.

Examples:
    a) “Why is climate change a global concern? Explain how greenhouse gases contribute to global warming.”
    b) “How do vaccines work? Describe the process of immunization in simple terms.”
    c) “What role does AI play in reducing carbon emissions? How can it help industries become more sustainable?”
    d) “Why is recycling important? How does AI improve waste management systems?”

4. Missing Word Prompting:
This technique uses fill-in-the-blank style prompts to test comprehension and reinforce learning.

Examples:
    a) “The capital of France is ___.”
    b) “In photosynthesis, plants absorb sunlight to produce ___.”
    c) “AI helps in predicting ___ changes to support climate research.”
    d) “Electric vehicles help reduce ___ emissions.”

### Conclusion 
From the above experiment, it is observed that various prompting techniques such as Straightforward, Tabular, Preceding Question, and Missing Word Prompting can be effectively utilized to generate structured, informative, and logical AI responses.
Each method plays a unique role — straightforward prompts give direct results, tabular prompts improve clarity, preceding question prompts enhance reasoning, and missing word prompts test understanding.
